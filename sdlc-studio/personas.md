# User Personas

## Marvin — Primary

**Role:** Owner and everyday user  
**Technical Proficiency:** Beginner  
**Primary Goal:** Store personal schedule information and retrieve it through a friendly voice assistant.

### Needs

- Clear controls with plain-language feedback
- A visible record of what JR remembers
- Easy correction and deletion of stored information
- A natural spoken response after saying “Hey JR”
- Privacy-conscious defaults

### Pain Points

- Applications that require technical setup
- Voice interfaces that listen without an obvious indicator
- Assistants that confidently invent information
- Data spread across unrelated applications

### Experience Goal

> “I want JR to feel personal and helpful while I stay in control of what it remembers.”

## Negative Persona — Passive Surveillance User

JR is not designed to secretly record people, store every conversation automatically, or collect information without clear consent.
